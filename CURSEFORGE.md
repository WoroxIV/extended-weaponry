# Extended Weaponry

**Copper, emerald, amethyst and Warden versions of every Better Weaponry weapon, plus full tool and armor sets in these materials.**

Requires [Better Weaponry](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/better-weaponry-mod) 3.1.0 or newer. Works with Combat Reforged, Better Combat and Epic Fight like the other Better Weaponry weapons.

## What it adds

Four materials, each with all 13 Better Weaponry weapons, a shield, a bow, a crossbow and shurikens, plus a sword, pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe and a full armor set. That makes 106 items, in their own creative tab.

- **Copper**: between stone and iron, with the same stats as the copper tools and armor of Minecraft 1.21.9. Early game for versions that do not have them yet.
- **Emerald**: a bit more durable than iron, hits between iron and diamond, and enchants very well.
- **Amethyst**: hits harder than diamond, but breaks very fast. Unbreaking and Mending do not work on it, it can never be made unbreakable, and it shatters into shards when it breaks. Its hits *resonate* and damage nearby enemies.
- **Warden**: an upgrade of netherite at the smithing table, stronger than netherite. Its hits pierce part of the armor and make no vibration.

## The Warden Heart

Each Warden piece needs a **Warden Heart**. A Warden drops one only if players dealt at least 60% of its damage, in melee or with arrows. TNT, lava, dripstone and other tricks do not count. The **Warden upgrade smithing template** is found in ancient city chests, and is duplicated with netherite scraps and an echo shard.

## Good to know

- Zombies and vindicators sometimes spawn with copper or emerald gear. Worn gear can also be found in a few chests.
- With Deeper and Darker installed, its own Warden gear is used, and the Warden gear of this mod is hidden.
- Available for NeoForge and Fabric 1.21.1, and Forge and Fabric 1.20.1.
- Translated into English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), Russian and Simplified Chinese.
