## 1.0.1

- New textures for every Warden item: weapons, bows and crossbows, tools, armor, shield, Warden Heart and upgrade template.
- Retouched copper, emerald and amethyst textures.
- Amethyst weapons no longer have Resonance.
- The "Fragile" and "Pierces Armor" lines now show right under the item name. "Fragile" is now pink.
- The mod's creative tab now comes after the vanilla tabs on NeoForge and Forge.
- The empty Warden Heart slot of the smithing table is now dark gray, like the vanilla icons.
- New logo and icon, and a fuller description in the mod list.
