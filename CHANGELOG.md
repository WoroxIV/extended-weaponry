# 1.0.0

First release. Requires Better Weaponry 3.1.0 or newer.

- Four materials: copper, emerald, amethyst and Warden. Each has every Better Weaponry weapon, shield, bow, crossbow and shuriken, plus a sword, tools and armor.
- Copper uses the values of the copper tools and armor of Minecraft 1.21.9.
- Emerald is slightly more durable than iron, with damage between iron and diamond.
- Amethyst breaks very fast (16 durability) but hits harder than diamond. It cannot get Unbreaking or Mending, cannot be made unbreakable, shatters into shards when it breaks, and its hits resonate on nearby enemies.
- Warden is an upgrade of netherite at the smithing table, with a template from ancient cities and a Warden Heart. Its hits pierce armor and make no vibration.
- The Warden Heart drops when players dealt at least 60% of the damage taken by the Warden. Explosions, fire, magic and thorns do not count.
- With Deeper and Darker, the Warden gear of this mod is hidden and cannot be obtained.
- Translations: English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Brazilian and European Portuguese, Russian, Simplified Chinese.
